About

I'm currently a postdoc researcher at the Brain and Learning lab (University of Geneva, Switzerland), with Daphne Bavelier, where I investigate the dynamic integration of perceptual and value-based decision-making, combining gamified behavioral tasks and computational modeling.

Before that, I did a short postdoc (2022-2023) in my PhD lab at the Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives et Computationnelles (Ecole normale supérieure, Paris, France) with Stefano Palminteri, where I investigated human moral alignment with Large Language Models across high-stakes decision-making contexts.

I did my PhD (2018-2022) at the Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives et Computationnelles - Institut Jean-Nicod in the Human Reinforcement Learning team, (Ecole normale supérieure, Paris, France), combining behavioral experiments and computational reinforcement learning models to examine how humans represent and integrate heterogeneous reward signals. I investigated the commensurability of subjective values derived from experiential trial-and-error versus explicit symbolic descriptions, characterizing the cognitive valuation mechanisms enabling multi-attribute comparison on a common scale.

Besides, I have interests in philosophy (e.g. philosophy of mind, epistemology, ethics), humanities in general (e.g. sociology, political science), as well as some fields related to programming and computer science (free software movement, web dev, game dev). I also play the guitar 😊.
